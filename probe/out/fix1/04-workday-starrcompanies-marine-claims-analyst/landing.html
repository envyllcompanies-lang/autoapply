This position is an mid level position into Marine Cargo claims. The suitable candidate will review and evaluate newly reported claims and work with their assigned manager in evaluating coverage, establishing appropriate reserves, undertaking claim investigations, overseeing TPA handling and managing expenses. Claims will be maintained and managed in accordance with the Company’s Claims Handling Guidelines.
