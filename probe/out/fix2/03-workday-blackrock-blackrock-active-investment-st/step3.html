*A Personal Relationship is defined as one that goes beyond the normal relationship between co-workers such that it may be viewed by a reasonable person as being susceptible to perceived impropriety, bias, favoritism and/or abuse of authority within a work environment. Examples of Personal Relationships include, but are not limited to, romantic or sexual relationships, substantial outside business relationships, and family relationships (as defined below).

A family relationship includes, but is not limited to, spouse, domestic partner, child (including adopted), parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle, niece or nephew, first cousin or corresponding in-law or “step” or “half” relation. For purposes of this policy, a family-like relationship (such as a godparent) is also considered a family relationship.
